THE STORY BEHIND THE SWAP

Skills are better shared.

SkillSwap AI is a student-built community where you teach what you know and learn something new from someone else. No payment. Just a fair exchange of skills.

Made by students, for students
👋
THE PEOPLE BEHIND IT

Built by students who wanted a better way to learn.

Mayank · Founder
Harsh · Co-founder

We’re building SkillSwap AI around one simple belief: everyone knows something worth teaching, and everyone has something left to learn.

🎯
OUR PURPOSE

Make learning a two-way street.

Help students find people with complementary skills, connect through a swap, and learn together without making money the barrier.

🔁
WHY IT’S DIFFERENT

Trade knowledge, not cash.

Instead of buying a lesson, offer a skill in return. Both people bring something to the table, so everyone gets to be both a learner and a teacher.

🧩
HOW IT WORKS

From a skill match to a real plan.

Create your profile, discover compatible people, send a swap request, then use a private Mission Room to chat, set goals, plan tasks and track progress.

🏆
GROW AS YOU GO

Progress deserves a little celebration.

Complete swaps, earn XP and see the community leaderboard grow. The point is not to compete harder, but to keep learning and helping others.

🛡️
COMMUNITY FIRST

Respect, safety and curiosity.

Keep personal details private, treat other members respectfully, and use the report and block tools if an interaction feels uncomfortable.
